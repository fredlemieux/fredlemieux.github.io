Privacy policy

PAI-greg is a personal automation tool used only by its owner. It accesses the owner's own Google account data (such as calendar, email, tasks, spreadsheets, files and selected photos) solely to perform tasks the owner requests.

  • Data is processed on the owner's own machine and is not sold, shared or transferred to third parties.
  • No data from any other person's Google account is accessed.
  • OAuth tokens are stored locally on the owner's machine and can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
